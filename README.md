# Justice for PUBG VN

During PUBG Asia Stars 2026, Vietnamese players Himass and TanVuu were accused of using livestream information during play. PUBG removed them from the event and, after an investigation, announced permanent bans from their game accounts and official esports events. KRAFTON also acknowledged problems with its guidance and early communication.

This independent website documents the September 2026 timeline, links to official notices and archived sources, and calls for a transparent review of the evidence and proportionate sanctions. It identifies which claims are official findings and which questions remain unresolved.
